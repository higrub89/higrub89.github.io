My Story

For a decade I worked as an electromechanical technician on high-performance platforms: Ferrari, Lamborghini, MV Agusta, Ducati, Honda, Yamaha, among others. The work included complete powertrain diagnostics, electronic fault isolation, high-voltage systems on V8/V10/V12 architectures, fuel injection calibration, and engine mapping. That experience taught me to work under extreme tolerances, where an incorrect diagnosis has real and immediate consequences.

I also worked in ILS (Integrated Logistics Support) engineering at Sonovision Ingenieros España, within tactical military vehicle programs. Technical authoring and validation of repair and maintenance documentation for platforms such as the VEMPAR and VPTL (Renault Trucks Defense), including electrical schematic interpretation and CAN-Bus network topology analysis.

From that foundation, I made a deliberate transition into systems-level software development. I currently study at 42 Madrid (Fundación Telefónica) and in the DAM (Cross-Platform Application Development, CIPFPD La Rioja). I work in C and C++ under strict compilation (-Wall -Wextra -Werror), with verification through Valgrind, ThreadSanitizer, and Helgrind. All projects go through CI pipelines with GitHub Actions before merge.

My goal is to build low-level software with the same discipline I learned diagnosing engines: deterministic memory management, zero race conditions, and code that is auditable from start to finish.
